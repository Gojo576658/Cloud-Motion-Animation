# "Two Guesses Left" — long video script (MechivioTech)

**Length:** ~7:45 (≈1,100 words of narration at a calm pace, plus dramatic pauses)
**Mood:** 70% suspense thriller (dark score, heartbeat, ticking, silence before reveals) + 30% light comedy
(the jokes land on the *drive* and on situations, never on Stefan; he is a real person who lost a fortune).
**Hooks:** 25 (marked **H1–H25**). Every hook ends a beat with a new open question, so there is never a
"safe moment" to leave. A new visual idea at least every 4–5 seconds.

**Working title:** `He Has 2 Guesses Left to Unlock Half a Billion Dollars`
Alt: `This USB Drive Will Destroy $580 Million If He Guesses Wrong`
**Thumbnail idea:** extreme close-up of the steel drive, its little screen glowing red **"2 TRIES LEFT"**,
a trembling finger over the Enter key. Text: **"2 TRIES LEFT"**.

## Fact sheet (checked; how each line is phrased)
- Stefan Thomas received **7,002 BTC** in 2011 for making the animated video "What is Bitcoin?"; keys stored on an **IronKey**;
  password written on paper that was lost; **8 of 10** guesses used. (NYT 2021, via TechRadar, Denver7/KGUN; Trakx)
- Quote "I would just lay in bed and think about it…" is from the **New York Times** (2021), as quoted by TechRadar/Nexstar.
- Value: ~**$220M** in early 2021. At ~**$82,800/BTC** (Oct 11, 2026) → **≈ $580M** ("more than half a billion"). At the
  **Oct 2025 peak (~$126,080)** → **≈ $880M**. Recheck the price on upload day and update the on-screen tag.
- **Kingston** (makes IronKeys today) said it cannot recover the data; no backup password. (TechRadar 2021)
- IronKey wipes after **10** wrong attempts (crypto-erase of its key).
- **Unciphered + Wired (Oct 2023):** test IronKey **S200**, chip removed, epoxy dissolved with nitric acid, ~**200 trillion**
  guesses, unlocked. Stefan **declined** because he had already hired another team for a share. No public confirmation the drive
  has been opened (as of this script). (Bitdefender, Benzinga, CCN, crypto.news)
- **Joe Grand / Dan Reich (2021, video Jan 2022):** Trezor One, ~**$2M in crypto** (Theta tokens, so say "crypto", not "bitcoin");
  **12** wrong guesses, wipe at **16**; wait time grows after each miss; firmware-update copies PIN to RAM; voltage
  **fault injection** to read RAM; ~**12 weeks**; Trezor says the flaw was fixed in **2017**. (Decrypt, Cointelegraph, FXEmpire)
- **James Howells:** says he threw away a drive with ~**8,000 BTC** in 2013; High Court dismissed his claim (Jan 2025), Court of
  Appeal refused permission (Mar 2025). (Wikipedia, Local Government Lawyer, ForkLog)
- **Chainalysis (2017):** 2.78M–3.79M BTC likely lost, **17–23%** of supply → "up to one in five". (Fortune, Cointelegraph)
- Stefan's own words: tweeted it was "a painful memory" and hopes others learn from his mistakes (Fox/Today); told n-tv he was
  depressed for a few weeks, then chose to look ahead and keep working (via Mein-MMO).

**Respect rules:** Stefan, Dan, Joe and James are real people. They are drawn as generic cartoons (no likeness), shown with
sympathy, and only reported facts are used. The humour targets Ike (the drive), robots, paper, and situations.
