# THE CURSED CPU — "He Bought a Used CPU… and Got BANNED"

Long-form explainer, 16:9, target ≈ 5:45. Funny + emotional, "how tech works" core.
Voice: Kokoro am_michael. Music: playful pizzicato/suspense score with comedy stings.
Motion personality: **Playful-cinematic** — characters move with cartoon timing (anticipation,
squash & stretch, overshoot springs, hard cuts on punchlines, freeze frames, zoom punches);
the tech explanation (kernel, TPM vault) slows into smooth cinematic camera moves so it reads
as "serious mode" — the contrast is part of the joke.

Facts are attributed the way the reporting does: the buyer's claims are "according to Leo",
Riot's side is quoted from its anti-cheat lead. "Leo" is a nickname — the real buyer is anonymous.

Sources: PC Gamer, Dexerto, Heise, Tom's Hardware, TechPowerUp, VideoCardz (Oct 2026);
Riot Support "Enable TPM 2.0"; Riot Vanguard (Wikipedia).
