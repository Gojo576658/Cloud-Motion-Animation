May 12, 2017. Hospital screens across England turn red. A worm called WannaCry is locking computers in more than 100 countries and demanding $300 in bitcoin. Then a 22-year-old researcher on his week off spots a strange, unregistered web address inside the code, buys it for $10.69, and the attack starts to switch itself off. Three months later, the FBI arrested him at the airport in Las Vegas.
